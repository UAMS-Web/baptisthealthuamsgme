# Baptist Health UAMS GME theme

This wiki documents the Baptist Health UAMS GME WordPress theme: a Genesis child theme based on Utility Pro. It covers installing the theme, setting up its pages, the widget areas, page templates and features it adds, and what to check when something does not show. Development, tests and the contribution process are in the repository's [CONTRIBUTING.md](https://github.com/UAMS-Web/baptisthealthuamsgme/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md).

**Getting started**

- [Installation](Installation.md): requirements, installing the theme beside Genesis, and activating it.
- [Setting up the home page](Setting-up-the-home-page.md): filling the home page's welcome, gallery and call-to-action sections.

**Reference**

- [Widget areas](Widget-areas.md): every widget area the theme registers, where it appears, and when.
- [Page templates](Page-templates.md): the Landing, Leadership and News templates, and the front page.
- [Theme features](Theme-features.md): menus, color styles, layouts, image sizes, scripts and suggested plugins.

**Troubleshooting**

- [Troubleshooting](Troubleshooting.md): a section that does not appear, a missing template, and other symptoms.

**Project**

- [Contributing](https://github.com/UAMS-Web/baptisthealthuamsgme/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md): the issue-to-merge path and test plans.
- [Repository](https://github.com/UAMS-Web/baptisthealthuamsgme): source, issues and releases.
